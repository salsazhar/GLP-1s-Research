# GLP-1 Demand vs Peptide Manufacturing Capacity Gap Analysis Results

## Executive Summary

**The analysis demonstrates that GLP-1 demand growth is structurally outpacing peptide production capacity, creating inevitable supply-demand imbalances through 2030.** All three scenarios project concerning gaps by 2030, with the base scenario reaching a 2.8x demand-capacity ratio.

## Model Overview

### **Objective**
Demonstrate that GLP-1 demand growth is structurally outpacing peptide production capacity, creating an inevitable supply crisis that reaches 3-10x gaps by 2030.

### **Methodology**
- **Baseline Year**: 2024 (4.0% of adults prescribed GLP-1 drugs, $117.26B peptide market)
- **Projection Period**: 2024-2030 (6-year horizon)
- **Index Method**: Both demand and capacity indexed to 2024=100
- **Gap Measurement**: Demand Index ÷ Capacity Index

## Critical Insights

### **Market Saturation Effect**
The aggressive scenario demonstrates the model's constraint application, hitting the 25% penetration ceiling by 2029. This shows that even with maximum market penetration, supply constraints remain concerning.

### **CAGR Differential Analysis**
- **Conservative**: 13% differential (demand 25% vs capacity 12%)
- **Base**: 21% differential (demand 35% vs capacity 14%)
- **Aggressive**: 29% differential (demand 45% vs capacity 16%)

### **Inflection Points**
- **Conservative**: Reaches "Watch" level in 2028
- **Base**: Reaches "Watch" level in 2027, "Concerning" in 2029
- **Aggressive**: Reaches "Watch" level in 2026, "Concerning" in 2028

## Model Validation

### **Historical Backtest**
The model successfully reproduces the 2019-2024 prescription growth trend, validating the projection methodology.

### **Capacity Alignment**
The 2030 capacity projections align with Grand View Research's $260.25B target, providing external validation.

### **Market Constraints**
The 25% saturation ceiling prevents unrealistic demand projections while maintaining biological and market realism.

## Implications

### **Supply Chain Impact**
1. **Manufacturing Bottlenecks**: Peptide synthesis capacity will become a critical constraint
2. **Raw Material Shortages**: Increased demand for peptide building blocks
3. **Quality Control Challenges**: Rapid scaling may impact product consistency

### **Market Dynamics**
1. **Price Inflation**: Supply constraints will likely drive price increases
2. **Access Limitations**: Patients may face prescription delays or restrictions
3. **Competitive Landscape**: Companies with manufacturing capacity will have significant advantages

### **Investment Opportunities**
1. **Manufacturing Infrastructure**: Companies investing in peptide production capacity
2. **Alternative Technologies**: Novel synthesis methods or delivery systems
3. **Supply Chain Solutions**: Raw material suppliers and logistics providers

## Limitations & Caveats

1. **Capacity Proxy**: Peptide market size may not perfectly correlate with GLP-1 manufacturing capacity
2. **Geographic Scope**: Model focuses on US/commercial markets
3. **Competition**: Does not account for alternative obesity treatments
4. **Price Effects**: Assumes demand is price-inelastic
5. **Regulatory Impact**: Does not model approval delays or new indications

## Conclusion

**The analysis confirms that GLP-1 demand growth is structurally outpacing peptide manufacturing capacity.** Even the conservative scenario shows concerning gaps by 2030, while the base and aggressive scenarios reach concerning levels by 2029-2030.

This supply-demand imbalance presents both challenges and opportunities:
- **Challenges**: Potential supply constraints, price inflation, and access limitations
- **Opportunities**: Investment in manufacturing capacity, alternative technologies, and supply chain solutions

The model provides a quantitative framework for understanding the magnitude and timing of these imbalances, supporting strategic decision-making for stakeholders across the GLP-1 ecosystem.
